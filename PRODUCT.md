# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The current project is intentionally lightweight and does not require an application framework.

## Users

Businesses, independent professionals, and organizations in Iraq and abroad that need a credible public-facing website and want to discuss scope before receiving a quote.

## Product Purpose

Codeylon is a Baghdad-based online agency that designs and builds public-facing websites. The website should explain what the agency can responsibly deliver, establish trust, set realistic time expectations, and convert suitable visitors into project enquiries.

## Positioning

Codeylon provides carefully scoped, custom front-end websites without presenting itself as a full-stack software studio. Every engagement begins with a project brief and a scope-based conversation rather than public package pricing.

## Operating Context

Prospects may arrive from search, the codeylon.com domain, or Codeylon's Instagram profile. They need to understand fit, timing, process, and limitations before contacting the agency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Builds marketing websites, landing pages, business websites, portfolios, campaign sites, and front-end redesigns.
- Can integrate straightforward third-party services such as forms, maps, analytics, and booking links.
- Does not currently take on database-backed products, account systems, authentication, or complex custom backend workflows.
- No prices are displayed publicly. Quotes are based on confirmed scope.
- Estimates are working-time ranges, not unconditional delivery guarantees.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Codeylon.
- Domain: codeylon.com.
- Instagram: codeylon.
- Based in Baghdad, Iraq.
- Preserve the incumbent navy, warm gold, and parchment identity while improving its usability and production quality.
- Voice should be direct, credible, and specific. Avoid inflated AI/startup language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing static website in `index.html`.
- Existing contact address in the website: hello@codeylon.com.
- No verified client logos, testimonials, awards, performance statistics, or case studies are available; future work must not fabricate them.
- No photography is required. Interface and diagrammatic visuals should be authored from the design system rather than filled with stock placeholders.

## Product Principles

- State scope and limitations clearly.
- Earn trust with process, detail, and working quality rather than invented social proof.
- Make contact easy without publishing generic prices.
- Treat accessibility, privacy, security, performance, and mobile behavior as part of the deliverable.
- Keep ownership and future editing practical through a simple static stack.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the public website, including keyboard access, visible focus, adequate contrast, reduced-motion support, clear form errors, and layouts that remain usable at 200% zoom.
