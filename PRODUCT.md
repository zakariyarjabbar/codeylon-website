# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, as explicitly required by the project brief. The site is a frontend-only deployment with no database, authentication, or custom backend.

## Users

The primary users are Iraqi business owners and decision-makers evaluating whether Codeylon can present their business professionally online. Typical visitors operate clinics, restaurants and cafés, service companies, real-estate businesses, local retail, salons, professional offices, startups, and personal brands. They need to understand the offer quickly, judge the studio's credibility, inspect representative work, and begin a project inquiry from a phone or desktop.

## Product Purpose

Codeylon's website explains and sells a focused studio service: clean, fast, responsive, Arabic-first business websites and frontend experiences. Success means a qualified visitor understands the scope, trusts the studio's process and craft, and prepares a useful quote request through WhatsApp, email, or a copied project summary.

## Positioning

Codeylon combines disciplined modern frontend craft with native Arabic/RTL thinking and a restrained Babylonian/Iraqi brand reference. It specializes in high-quality business-facing websites rather than claiming broad SaaS, platform, dashboard, authentication, or database capabilities.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from Instagram, referrals, or direct sharing, often on mobile. The site must support browsing services and conceptual portfolio work, understanding process and service boundaries, reviewing legal information, and generating a frontend-only quote request that can be handed off through existing communication channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Core services: landing pages, business websites, multi-page company websites, presentation and portfolio sites, marketing pages, redesigns, and frontend implementation.
- Not a core offer: authentication, database-heavy products, dashboards, complex backend platforms, SaaS, marketplaces, ERP systems, or advanced web applications.
- Arabic is the default language and the entire information architecture is RTL-first.
- Portfolio content is maintained in `/data/portfolio.json` and powers listing, featured, and dynamic detail pages.
- Quote and contact flows have no backend; they validate locally and prepare WhatsApp, email, and copyable summaries.
- No public pricing, invented awards, false client claims, fake testimonials, fake enterprise logos, or unsupported results.
- Exact private business details that were not supplied may use realistic, internally consistent mock contact data as authorized by the brief.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Codeylon, combining Code and Babylon.
- Domain: `codeylon.com`; Instagram: `@codeylon`.
- Supplied official mark: `Group4444.svg` (transparent SVG, replacing the initial `Group33.png`). Website asset: `public/brand/codeylon-mark.svg`; original paths and `#BD9A41` fill preserved, with only empty canvas trimmed.
- Binding palette origin: deep Babylonian navy and warm brass/gold from the supplied mark, supported by sand, ivory, charcoal, and slate neutrals.
- Voice: natural professional Arabic, clear, commercially credible, restrained, and honest.
- Visual commitment: photography-led, editorial, structured, quietly confident, locally relevant, and free of generic SaaS/AI-agency styling.

## Evidence on Hand

- The supplied Codeylon logo mark is verified brand evidence.
- No verified awards, testimonials, customer metrics, major-client logos, press features, or long company history were supplied and none may be fabricated.
- The brief references a real printing-shop project but supplies no verified project facts or imagery; portfolio examples may therefore be labeled transparently as concepts or presentation models.

## Product Principles

1. Make the real service boundary clearer, never broader.
2. Earn trust through specificity, process, craft, and honest labeling rather than inflated claims.
3. Design for Arabic reading behavior and Iraqi business context from the first layout decision.
4. Make every conversion path useful without pretending a backend exists.
5. Preserve editorial restraint: photography and content lead; interface decoration recedes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must use semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation and dialogs, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, descriptive alternative text, accessible form feedback, and responsive layouts from small mobile screens through large desktops.
